# Logo

Use the primary logo (JCI wordmark plus shield, with the TM mark) in almost every application. Use the `Logo` component, or the files in `assets/Logos`.

## Versions

- **Primary**: the JCI wordmark and shield. Default choice.
- **Secondary**: adds "Junior Chamber International" beneath. For letterheads, email signatures, formal documents and first introductions. Not supplied here as a file; obtain it from JCI.
- **National and local**: the primary logo with the official location name below, left-aligned, in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold in `jci-blue`. Use the `LocalLogo` component. The name never extends past the shield's first outer line on line one nor past the shield on line two; split longer names over two lines, and shrink a single overlong word. Official logos can be generated at https://jci.cc/logo-generator/.
- **Shield icon**: alone only when legibility takes priority (profile images, favicons, small merchandise).

## Colourways

Choose the version from the background first.

1. **Default** (`jci-logo-default.png`): on white or light backgrounds.
2. **Inverted** (`jci-logo-inverted.png`): on `jci-black` or other dark backgrounds.
3. **White** (`jci-logo-white.png`): on coloured backgrounds such as `jci-blue`, and on dark photography.
4. **Black** (`jci-logo-black.png`): single-colour or technical cases, and light photography.

Default and black are the supplied master files. White and inverted were derived by recolouring those masters to the palette; replace them with JCI's own files when available. For the secondary and local logos the name follows the colour of the JCI acronym, except that jci-blue is preferred for the location name in default and inverted versions.

## Clearspace and size

- Keep clear space on every side equal to half the width of the shield icon. Nothing may encroach.
- Never reproduce the logo below 5 mm high in print or 32 px high on screen.

## Placement

Preferred: top left and top right. Optional: bottom left and bottom right, when imagery or headlines occupy the top. Last resort: top or bottom centre. Always keep clearspace, avoid crowding and sit on a background with full contrast.

## Never

Do not scale elements individually, stretch, rotate, change the distance between elements, flip the shield, change the layout, recolour, outline, or add effects such as shadows.
