JCI (Junior Chamber International) is a global network of emerging leaders who learn by doing, grow by sharing and lead by acting. This system encodes the JCI Brand Guidelines v1.4 (18 December 2025): colours, type, logo, ripple and photography. Its tagline is **Become the Change.**

## Content fundamentals

- Write in an active, encouraging voice that addresses young leaders directly ("Your engagement results in becoming better through learning by doing"). Speak as "we" for JCI.
- Build every message on one of three core messages: **Focus on Real Impact** (meaningful impact on members and communities), **Developing Leadership Skills** (better through learning by doing), **Diversity of Opportunities** (four areas of opportunity and a sense of belonging). They are jumping-off points, not scripts.
- Sign off with **Become the Change.** Headlines are sentence case ("Developing leaders").
- Never use emoji as decoration.

## Where to look

- `logo.md`: logo versions, colourways, clearspace, placement, what never to do.
- `colour.md`: palette, proportions, approved pairings and contrast.
- `typography.md`: Plus Jakarta Sans, Arvo, fallbacks and the type hierarchy.
- `ripple-and-photography.md`: the ripple motif and photography direction.

## Visual foundations

- Ground: `surface` (white) with `surface-tint` panels in light; `surface` (jci-black) in dark. Keep white or jci-black for about 40% of a layout, `jci-blue` for about 35%, and `jci-navy` and `jci-teal` for about 10% each. Use `jci-yellow` only as a 5% accent.
- Body text is `ink` set in `paragraph`. Headlines use `title`, subheads use `subheading`. Use `accent-text` (not jci-blue) for small accent-coloured text on light grounds.
- Corners of panels follow `radius-md`; spacing follows the `space-*` scale (both are derived, the guide does not define them).
- The signature graphic is the ripple: concentric, quartered rings, each rotated 15 degrees. Use the `Ripple` component, large and cropped by the layout edge.
- Photography is candid, warm and people-first.

## Iconography

JCI defines no icon set. The only pictogram is the shield icon (the logo without the wordmark), reserved for profile images, favicons and small merchandise where the full logo cannot fit. Do not invent icon families in the JCI style; use plain type or the shield.
