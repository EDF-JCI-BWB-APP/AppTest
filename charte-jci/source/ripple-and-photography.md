# Ripple and photography

## Ripple

The ripple, taken from the logo's concentric lines, is the central graphic motif. Use the `Ripple` component. It is used subtly in backgrounds or as a distinctive large element (slides, stationery, posters), usually bleeding off an edge.

- Concentric rings, each split into four equal quarters.
- The gap between quarters equals the ring width, and the space between rings equals the ring width.
- Each ring is rotated 15 degrees relative to the next smaller ring.
- Extend by adding rings outward with the same spacing and line width.
- Colour the four quarters from the palette (teal, blue, navy and black on a light ground); add faded outer rings for depth. Keep to the approved colour pairings.

## Photography

- **Focus on people**: faces, expressions and interaction.
- **Candid and authentic**: in-the-moment rather than posed or over-polished.
- **Diversity and representation**: members of different backgrounds, regions and experiences.
- **Energy and optimism**: bright, positive, natural light, open compositions.
- On photography, use the white or black logo version according to the image.
