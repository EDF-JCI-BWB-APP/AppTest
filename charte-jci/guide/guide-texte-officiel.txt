     Primary Logo


     The primary logo is the main visual identifier of
     our brand and should be used in the majority of
     applications. It consists of a shield icon paired
     with our JCI wordmark.

     Consistent use of the primary logo reinforces
     familiarity, strengthens brand recognition, and
     ensures a unified identity across all touchpoints.

     Construction


     The shield icon is constructed from three sets
     of concentric circles, carefully aligned to form
     a symmetrical and visually balanced shape.

     This geometric foundation ensures that the
     logo feels consistent, proportionate, and
     well-balanced at any scale.

     the logo should never be altered or distorted.
     Each element is deliberately designed to
     maintain visual harmony and brand recognition.

     Shield Icon

     the wordmark, in rare circumstances where
     limited space makes the full logo impractical.
     This includes applications such as social

     the primary representation of JCI. The icon is
     reserved for moments when legibility takes
     priority.

     Clearspace


     To maintain legibility and visual impact, the         Minimum size
     logo must always be surrounded by sufficient           5mm / 32px    JCI ~
     clearspace. This space ensures the logo is not
     crowded or compromised by other graphic
     elements, text, or edges.

     For all logo variations, the minimum clearspace
     on every side of the logo is equal to half the
     width of the shield icon. No other elements
     should encroach on this area.

     To preserve legibility, the logo should never
     be reproduced smaller than 5 mm in height for
     print, or 32 px in height for digital applications.

     possible. It’s intended for placement on light or
     neutral backgrounds where contrast is strong.

     Non-Latin Typeface




                                                         Noto Sans
     While Plus Jakarta Sans is the primary brand
     typeface, it does not support non-Latin
     writing systems. To ensure consistency and
     accessibility across all regions and languages,
     we recommend using Noto Sans for all non-
     Latin text.

     Noto Sans is part of the Google Fonts library
     and offers a comprehensive family of typeface
     variations covering the most widely used
     languages and writing systems worldwide.
     Its neutral, modern design aligns well with the
     look and feel of Plus Jakarta Sans, allowing for
     a cohesive typographic experience across
     languages.

     Whenever Latin and non-Latin text appear
     together, use Plus Jakarta Sans for Latin scripts
     and the appropriate Noto Sans variant for the
     non-Latin scripts, maintaining a balanced and
     unified layout.

     Logo placement


     Consistent placement strengthens recognition
     and creates a clear visual structure across all of
     our materials.

     The logo works best when positioned along the
     outer edge of a layout, while always respecting
     the required minimum clearspace.

     PREFERRED POSITIONS
     The top left and top right are the primary and
     most effective placement options. These
     positions align with typical reading patterns,
     and provide a strong starting point for the rest
     of the layout.

     OPTIONAL POSITIONS                                   PREFERRED Top left     IF NECESSARY Top centre      PREFERRED Top right
     The bottom left and bottom right corners may
     be used when the preferred positions conflict
     with imagery, headline pleacement, or other
     essential content.

     LAST-RESORT POSITIONS
     The top centre and bottom centre positions
     should be used only when none of the corner
     placements are feasible. Centre placement
     draws more attention and can disrupt the visual
     balance of a composition if overused, so it
     should remain an exception rather than the rule.

     Wherever it appears, the logo must always
     maintain its clearspace, avoid crowding, and
     remain positioned on a background that
     ensures full legibility and contrast.

     circles, with each ring divided into four equal      x
     quarters. The spacing between each quarter is
     the same as the width of the ring.                   x


     SPACING AND SCALE
     The spacing between each ring is the same as
     the width of the ring itself. The ripple can be
     expanded by adding additional rings to the
     outisde, following the same spacing and line
     width rules.                                             15°


     ROTATION
     Each ring is rotated by 15 degrees relative to the
     ring one size smaller. This rotation introduces
     subtle movement, making the entire asset feel
     more dynamic.

     Photography


     Photography plays a key role in expressing the
     energy, diversity, and real-world impact of our
     organisation. The goal is to tell genuine stories,
     not stage perfect snapshots.

     FOCUS ON PEOPLE
     People should be at the centre of our
     photography. Faces, expressions, and
     interactions are important, as they highlight the
     community connection that defines JCI.

     CANDID AND AUTHENTIC MOMENTS
     Favour candid, in-the-moment photography
     over posed, overly polished shots. Images
     should feel natural and unstaged, reflecting
     real experiences. Authenticity builds trust and
     makes our organisation more inviting.

     DIVERSITY AND REPRESENTATION
     Our imagery should reflect the global, inclusive
     natur of JCI. Show members of different
     backgrounds, regions, and experiences.
     Diversity reinforces the sense of belonging and
     the global impact of our organisation.

     ENERGY AND OPTIMISM
     Aim for imagery that feels bright, positive, and
     forward-looking. Lighting should be natural
     where possible, and compositions should feel
     open and inviting.
